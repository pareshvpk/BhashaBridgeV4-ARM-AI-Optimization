§9Cross-device comparison

This device (highlighted) against the other two iPhones and the Android flagship (Galaxy S26 Ultra). Same sentence, same INT8 model. Lower is better for latency.

Long-sentence median latency — ms (lower better)
Throughput — Android-suite-compatible tok/s (higher better)
The Android S26 Ultra is ~2–2.5× faster on the same workload — it runs 2 threads on 8 uniform cores, while every iPhone here is single-threaded on 2 performance cores. Among the iPhones, the base iPhone 16 (iOS 27.0) leads and the iPhone 16 Pro Max (iOS 18.7.8) trails — a thermal + OS effect, not a hardware ranking: the Pro Max hit “fair” thermal and unplugged mid-run (drift +11.1%), while the iPhone 17 was the most stable (drift +0.2%).
